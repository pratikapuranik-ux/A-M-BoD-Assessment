# Outside-in anchor overrides

These anchors apply **only when the assessment is outside-in**. They restate two indicators around signals that are visible from public sources. The level-neutral anchors in `framework.md` are unchanged and remain the reference for self-assessment and inside-out levels. Scripts read these overrides from `assets/framework.json` (`outside_in_overrides`) and show them in the dashboard info button and in Appendix D.

## 1.2 Unmediated customer and frontline exposure

Availability outside-in: **Low-Medium** (framework: Low).

| Score | Outside-in anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | No disclosure of director or senior-leader customer or frontline engagement, and no customer-side experience on the board. |
| 3 | Disclosed site visits, customer events or a director with recent customer-facing executive experience, without evidence of findings returning to the board. |
| 5 | Disclosed structured programme (e.g. customer advisory board reporting to the board, regular director market visits) with findings reported to the board. |

## 4.1 Reversible decisions: classified, delegated, fast

Availability outside-in: **Low-Medium** (framework: Low-Medium).

| Score | Outside-in anchor |
|---|---|
| 1 | No delegation-of-authority disclosure; announced initiatives persist for years without visible scale-up or stop. |
| 3 | Delegation disclosed in general terms; some initiatives visibly scaled or stopped within 12-18 months. |
| 5 | Matters reserved and delegations disclosed; repeated public examples of pilots scaled or stopped within 12 months, without restructuring charges or blame. |

**Sources:** Governance guidelines; transcripts tracked quarter to quarter; press releases
